# ARTales typographic wordmark options board v0.1

## Review status

This directory contains a **review-only options board** for comparing six broad typographic
directions for “ARTales”. Its status is `review_only`, its approval state is
`awaiting_human_visual_review`, and it is explicitly not a master, candidate, or lockup.

No option is approved and no exact font has been selected. The board creates no wordmark
master, wordmark candidate, light/dark lockup candidate, lockup master, export, or public asset.
The locked standalone symbol is not shown, copied, or changed.

## Options and font strategy

The board compares classic literary, refined editorial, humanist/book, transitional, elegant
high-contrast, and neutral fallback serif directions. Every option displays exactly “ARTales”
at a consistent comparison size and identifies its font-family stack in helper text.

All samples remain live text and use only generic or system fallback stacks. No font is
committed, vendored, embedded, downloaded, or externally referenced. Availability varies by
viewer, so glyph forms, width, metrics, kerning, spacing, and placement may differ across
environments. A listed stack describes a review direction, not an approved exact font.

## Human review

Reviewers should choose a preferred option or reject all, then assess literary/editorial feel,
readability, distinctiveness, uppercase/lowercase balance, kerning, and spacing. They may also
consider conceptual fit beside the locked symbol, but must not treat this board as a lockup.

Reviewers must decide whether to request more directions or proceed to exact font selection,
provenance, and license review. Only after that separate review and explicit approval may a
real wordmark candidate or outlines be prepared.
