# ARTales light/dark lockup candidates v0.3

These horizontal light and dark lockups are **v0.3 review-only candidates**. They respond to human feedback that the v0.2 symbol still looked too small by increasing its total wrapper scale from `1.18` to `1.31`, toward a visual occupied-height target of approximately 20 grid units. Up to approximately 21 grid units and a slight downward optical overhang are acceptable review outcomes.

## Candidate files

- `artales-lockup-light.candidate.v0.3.svg` — locked gold symbol with Text Dark wordmark for review on Paper (`#FDF3E2`).
- `artales-lockup-dark.candidate.v0.3.svg` — locked gold symbol with Primary Gold wordmark for review on Deep Dark (`#141414`) or Ink Night (`#0F1315`).
- `artales-lockup-light-dark.candidate.v0.3.json` — provenance, exact v0.2/v0.3 composition parameters, limitations, and review state.

Both SVGs contain self-contained outlined geometry on transparent backgrounds. They have no live text, images, external references, or font dependency.

## Composition and review status

V0.3 keeps the v0.2 top translation, lets the enlarged symbol extend downward, and preserves the exact 144-unit viewport gap by moving the unchanged wordmark right only by the symbol's added scaled width. The v0.1 and v0.2 packages remain unchanged as historical review candidates. The locked source masters and their geometry are unchanged.

These files remain `lockup_candidate_review_only`, `awaiting_human_visual_review`, and `not_master: true`. This is not a lockup master, and no runtime or public integration is approved. Black or monochrome symbol usage is deferred to a later export profile after lockup approval; it is not part of primary light/dark lockup approval.
