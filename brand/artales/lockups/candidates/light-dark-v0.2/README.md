# ARTales light/dark lockup candidates v0.2

These horizontal light and dark lockups are **v0.2 review-only candidates**. They respond to human feedback by enlarging the symbol so its visual height approximately matches the locked wordmark's capital A. The symbol remains left of the wordmark, and the established 144-unit gap is preserved.

## Candidate files

- `artales-lockup-light.candidate.v0.2.svg` — locked gold symbol with Text Dark wordmark for review on Paper (`#FDF3E2`).
- `artales-lockup-dark.candidate.v0.2.svg` — locked gold symbol with Primary Gold wordmark for review on Deep Dark (`#141414`) or Ink Night (`#0F1315`).
- `artales-lockup-light-dark.candidate.v0.2.json` — provenance, v0.1/v0.2 composition parameters, limitations, and review state.

Both SVGs are self-contained outlined geometry on transparent backgrounds. They have no live text, images, external references, or font dependency.

## Review status and history

The v0.1 package remains unchanged as a historical review candidate. The locked symbol and wordmark masters are also unchanged: v0.2 only changes wrapper transforms and the candidate viewBox. These files remain `lockup_candidate_review_only`, `awaiting_human_visual_review`, and `not_master: true`; no lockup master or runtime/public integration is approved.

Review whether the larger symbol now carries the desired emphasis, matches the capital A optically, remains vertically balanced, and retains comfortable spacing. A black or monochrome symbol treatment is deferred; if needed, it can be defined later as a separate export profile rather than as part of primary light/dark lockup approval.
