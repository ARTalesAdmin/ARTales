# ARTales — general text eligibility gate v1

The source of truth is the **selected text version** (not the title of the original work).

1. Verify named original author(s), corroborated death years, and CZ/EU life + 70 term. For multiple authors require every contributor term to have expired. Unknown facts block automatic inclusion.
2. Determine whether selected source is original, translation, adaptation or unknown. A translation is a separately protected creative contribution; check each credited translator's death year. A living or still-protected translator invalidates **this source text** even when the original is public domain. Suggest another older translation or the original; do not ingest the protected translation.
3. Handle edition-specific creative text separately. Separable headers, editor's notes, Gutenberg boilerplate, wrappers, covers and illustrations are omitted from literary blocks and retained in immutable source evidence. Inseparable new creative additions or uncertain text identity require another source or review.
4. Assert byte-for-byte archived SHA-256, complete nonoverlapping component spans, UTF-8, capture timestamp and checked timestamp. Only allow covered, identified literary text components. Metadata parsed from Gutenberg (Author, Translator, Illustrator, Editor, Language) is **evidence to verify**, not legally conclusive. Missing/contradictory facts fail closed.
5. Return `eligible_for_editorial_draft`, `find_alternative_source` or `needs_facts`. Brief snapshot contains source URL, digest, capture/check times, original author/translator references, component digests, included/excluded IDs and reason codes. Eligibility **never** sets publication authorization.

The reusable pure module is `lib/rights/sourceTextEligibility.ts`. The Alice PG #11 adapter `lib/pilots/aliceTextOnlyRightsGate.ts` now invokes it for an actual pinned 173595-byte source. Real catalog ingest must supply verified source bytes, evidence-supported author/translator facts and component inventory through a trusted server capture; **this PR does not yet wire the generic gate into the persisted candidate-to-draft transaction**. Do not promote a draft solely from client/model-supplied metadata or a source URL.

Limitations: this is a deterministic CZ/EU screening rule, not a universal treatment of all works, neighboring rights or unusual legal regimes. Original author term verification cannot automatically establish translation provenance. A version with unknown credits cannot be auto-cleared just because the Gutenberg entry omits them. Human review is reserved for missing/contradictory rights facts or genuinely complex cases, not mandatory by default for clearly public-domain original text.
